Hi, I'm Santosh Gitay—an application architect, dad of two girls, and the person behind InfinitiLogicSolutions.

On weekends, I team up with AI agents to turn everyday “wouldn't it be useful if…” ideas into small, practical projects. I bring the ideas, architecture, and judgment; my AI teammates help me explore, build, and refine.

From a quick QR code to a document scan or a calmer way to organize the day, I love making tools that are simple to use. Your support is a kind vote for more of that.
